War of the 4 Kings: Support

War of the 4 Kings is a free card game for iPhone. You play against computer opponents, and no internet connection is needed.

Need help or found a problem?

Email support@luminousbluemedia.com. Please tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what happened. We read every message.

Learning to play

Tap How to play on the home screen of the app.

Your saved game

The game saves automatically on your phone. If you delete the app, the saved game is deleted with it.
