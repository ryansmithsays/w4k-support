Privacy Policy

This page covers two iPhone apps from luminousblue Media: War of the 4 Kings and Check My List. Neither app collects anything about you.

War of the 4 Kings

Last updated: October 22, 2026

The short version: the app collects nothing about you.

What the app stores on your phone

Your player name, your army choice, your Options, and your game in progress. This stays on your phone. We cannot see it, and it is not sent anywhere.

What the app does not do

It has no accounts, ads, purchases or analytics. It does not track you or use your location, contacts, photos or camera. It has no internet features.

Third parties

None. No outside company receives any information from the app.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, and everything it stored is removed.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Check My List

Last updated: October 25, 2026

The short version: the app collects nothing about you. Everything it uses stays on your phone.

What the app stores on your phone

Your routines and checklists, what you have checked off, and any changes you make to the built-in routines. This stays on your phone. We cannot see it, and it is not sent anywhere.

Your calendar

The app asks permission to read your calendar so it can build prep checklists and reminders around your events. It only reads your calendar on your phone. It never changes your calendar and does not send your calendar information anywhere.

Reminders and alarms

The app asks permission to show notifications and ring alarms so a reminder can stay on your screen until you deal with it. These are set up and run on your phone.

What the app does not do

It has no accounts, ads or analytics. It does not track you or use your location, contacts, photos or camera.

Third parties

None. No outside company receives any information from the app.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app, and everything it stored is removed. Your calendar is not affected, because it stays in your Calendar app.

Changes

If this ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
